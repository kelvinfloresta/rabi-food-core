# Product Context

Who the product is for and how it positions itself. Specific decisions live in `docs/decisions/`; read the ones relevant to the task.

## Customer profiles

**Discoverer** — does not know the product yet. Needs education before committing. Arrives via search, social media, or word of mouth.

**Decider** — already knows what they want. Needs minimum friction. Arrives via QR code at the establishment or a link shared by another customer.

## Positioning

A subscription model, fundamentally different from what the market is used to, built under extreme uncertainty (lean startup). Its value:

- Convenience and organization
- More competitive pricing
- Fresh food
- Helps local businesses generate value for their region: reduces dependency on loans, avoids abusive interest rates, fair pricing with no per-sale commission — unlike marketplaces that exploit local businesses. This is a core differentiator and needs further elaboration.
